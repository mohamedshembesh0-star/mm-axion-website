# 10 things we learned from cold outreach at Haelyx

Haelyx sends hundreds of cold messages a week to doctors (Instagram, email, website contact forms). These are the lessons that changed our reply rate the most. They apply 1:1 to selling websites to local businesses.

## 1. Open with them, not with you
"I was looking for coffee and found you" is about you. Start with something only they would recognise: their name, their street, a post of theirs, a number from their profile. The first line decides if they keep reading.

## 2. Offer something free they can SEE, not "ideas"
"Can I send you two ideas?" gets a polite maybe. "Can I make you a free mockup of your homepage?" gets a yes, because people want to see their own business looking better. Our best reply rates came from offering one free, finished piece of work.

## 3. Build the free thing from THEIR real stuff
Our worst result ever: a doctor said our sample looked "generic, like AI made it". It used stock images and a template look. Since then every sample uses the client's own photos, logo, colours and words. If the mockup could belong to any business, it will be ignored.

## 4. No price and no link in the first message
The first message has one job: get a reply. Price comes only after they have seen the mockup and like it.

## 5. Short and plain
3 to 5 short sentences. No "innovative solutions", no "we help businesses grow". Write it the way you would text a friend's shop.

## 6. One easy question at the end
End with a question that takes 2 seconds to answer: "Want me to send it?" Not "let me know your thoughts".

## 7. Give them an easy way out
We end first messages with: "if it's not for you, just say so and I won't write again". It sounds like it would lower replies; it raises them, and people who say no stay friendly.

## 8. Use the channel they actually check
Instagram DMs to businesses often sit in "requests" for weeks. For us, the website contact form and email got owners to reply within hours (staff forward them to the owner). Use the contact form or email on their site first, Instagram second.

## 9. Follow up, but never repeat yourself
Most yeses come after the 2nd or 3rd message. Follow up on day 2, day 4 and day 7, one short new line each time (never the same text twice). Stop the moment they say no.

## 10. Be fast once they say yes
Deliver the mockup within 24 hours of their yes. Speed is proof you will be good to work with. After they see it: one price, one link, one start date. No discounts.
