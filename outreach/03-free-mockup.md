# The free mockup: what it is and how it should look

The free mockup is your best salesperson. Its job: make the owner think "that's my business, and it looks better than I imagined". If it does that, the price conversation is easy.

## What it includes (one page only)
- **Their real brand:** their logo, their colours, their own photos (taken from their Instagram or Google profile). No stock photos, no "lorem ipsum", no placeholder people.
- **Their real information:** name, address with a Google Map, opening hours, phone, and the thing your first message promised (for a café: the menu; for a shop: products and prices; for a clinic: services and booking).
- **The problem fixed:** if your message said "tourists can't find your menu on Google", the menu must be right there on the homepage, in Polish and English.
- **Mobile first:** most owners will open it on their phone. Check it on a phone before sending.
- **A "preview" label** in a corner, and keep it out of Google (noindex). It is a preview, not their live site.

## What it does NOT include
- No extra pages, no booking system, no online shop. One strong homepage is enough to win the yes.
- No custom domain. Host it on a free preview link (for example `nazwa-preview.vercel.app`).

## How long it should take
- Aim for 1 to 2 hours of work, by reusing your own template and personalising the top section, colours, photos and the key info.
- Send it within 24 hours of their "yes". Speed sells.

## How to send it
1. One short message: "here's your homepage, made from your own photos and menu: <link>"
2. One line about what it fixes: "the menu and hours are now one tap away, in Polish and English"
3. Then the offer, once: "if you like it, I'll put it live on your own domain for <price>, ready in <X> days. Want me to start?"

## Rules
- Nothing goes live without their OK.
- Price only after they have seen it. One price, one payment link, one start date. No discounts; if price is a problem, change what's included (fewer pages), not the price per page.
- If they say no, thank them and leave the door open. Never send the same follow-up twice.
